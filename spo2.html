SpO₂ probe placement: RIGHT hand or RIGHT wrist (pre-ductal — reflects O₂ reaching brain and heart BEFORE ductus arteriosus).
Reading accuracy: Allow 60–90 seconds after probe application for stable signal.
Preterm <32w: Avoid SpO₂ >95% — hyperoxia causes retinopathy. Reduce FiO₂ immediately.
Free-flow O₂: Cup hand around nose/mouth. Do NOT use BVM for free-flow.
CPAP: For spontaneous breathing with increased work — PEEP 5–7 cmH₂O.
